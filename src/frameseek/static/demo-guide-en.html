Ask a question. Inspect the frame. Replay the evidence.

FrameSeek is a desktop film room for investigating basketball footage. Questions, cited source frames and follow-ups stay together in a conversation and a spatial canvas.

Start basketball demo · Back to FrameSeek

Synthetic basketball demo: these are generated stills with preset answers. No model is called. The sequence does not establish continuous play, player identity, or whether a shot scores.

Try the demo

  1. Open the basketball demo above on a desktop browser.
  2. Select What is visible in the pass scene? and send the question.
  3. Select Replay on the cited frame to seek to 00:06.
  4. Select Where is the defender in the shot scene? and send the follow-up. Replay its frame at 00:13.
  5. Choose Does the shot score? to see an explicitly uncertain answer. Open Canvas to inspect the connected questions; refresh to restore the investigation.

Sports and NVIDIA/Nebius evidence

A previous limited basketball test used NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Super VL through Nebius Token Factory and returned scoreboard evidence at source time 05:26.1 with a contextual follow-up. Those saved results were partial and are separate from this synthetic demo.

Uploaded-video analysis requires a configured processor and its existing authorization. The model and budget status is available under More → Models & budget.

What the demo does not prove

The preset answer is not a new model response. A sampled video index can miss brief appearances, and a cited frame is not proof of a complete event count or a person’s identity. The live NVIDIA/Nebius path and fresh-user real-video experience require separate verification.
