About this preview

A visual example, not a game analysis.

The basketball scenes and timestamps are illustrative. They are not model output or evidence from a real game.

The workspace offers a synthetic basketball demo with preset answers and no model calls. Uploaded videos require a configured analysis route and any required approvals.

The details / 03

Questions before you start.

What to expect when you open FrameSeek.

What can I try right now?

The workspace opens with a labeled synthetic demo and preset answers. The landing page preview is also illustrative. Neither sends model requests.

Can I use my own video?

Yes. You can upload video for local preflight and private playback. Uploads are subject to workspace limits; see the guide for more context.

How do I check an answer?

Use the cited timestamps and frames to return to the source video and inspect the surrounding footage. Review the evidence yourself before drawing a conclusion.

Does my uploaded video automatically get AI answers?

No. Uploaded-video analysis needs an appropriately configured route and its required approvals. The default fixture mode does not send model requests or substitute demo answers for uploaded footage.
